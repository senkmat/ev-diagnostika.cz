Usually a few minutes after the test, still on the spot, you get a certificate with the battery health, real-world range and a comparison with similar cars. I'll explain what the result means for the price.

What you'll learn: battery health and real range

  • Battery health (SoH) calculated independently by AVILOO, not just copied from the car's computer
  • Capacity and real-world range compared to the car when new (WLTP)
  • Market comparison – how the battery compares with cars of the same model and age
  • Check of sensors, BMS and individual cell voltages
  • QR code to verify the certificate is genuine

Example from a real test: Tesla Model 3 Long Range, 143,568 km – SoH 86.4 %, no anomalies. The car itself (BMS) reported only 79 %.

FAQ

How long does the test take?

The AVILOO FLASH measurement itself takes just a few minutes; the whole visit including setup is about 15–20 minutes. The certificate is usually ready a few minutes after the measurement, so you normally get it on the spot.

Does the car need to be driven or charged?

No. The FLASH test is done on a parked car via the OBD diagnostic port. There's no need to charge the battery to 100 % or run it down.

What's the difference between the FLASH and PREMIUM tests?

FLASH is a quick test on a parked car – the measurement takes a few minutes and you usually get the certificate on the spot. According to AVILOO it is accurate to about ±3 % compared with the PREMIUM test. For deciding whether to buy a used car it's enough in most cases.

PREMIUM is AVILOO's most detailed test. The battery is measured while driving as it gradually discharges from a high to a low state of charge, and the certificate arrives within 2 working days. It's useful for example in a dispute about the battery or when FLASH shows something suspicious. I don't offer it as standard yet – if you're interested, get in touch and we'll arrange it individually.

Isn't an OBD reader and a phone app enough?

The app shows the value the car calculates itself (BMS) – that's the manufacturer's estimate and may not match reality. AVILOO calculates battery health independently using data from thousands of comparable cars, checks individual cells too and issues a certificate that buyers or sellers can verify via QR code.

Which cars can be tested?

Most electric cars and plug-in hybrids. Mild hybrids can't be tested. You'll find the list of supported models in the AVILOO database – or send me the model and year and I'll check for you.

I'm buying the car from someone else. Is that possible?

Yes, that's the most common case. I come straight to the seller – they just need to agree to the diagnostics being connected. If you can't be there, I'll test the car without you and we'll go through the result by phone.

How soon can you come?

Usually the next day, weekends included. If you're in a hurry because someone else is interested in the car, call me – it's often possible the same day.
